Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Template — have a lawyer review before launch.

What we collect

  • Contact details you enter at checkout (name, phone, address) to fulfil orders.
  • Order history stored on your device (localStorage) and, once the backend is live, on our servers.
  • Newsletter email if you subscribe.

What we never collect

Card numbers, UPI PINs or passwords. Payments are processed by our gateway provider (Razorpay); we only receive a payment reference.

Your rights (India, DPDP Act 2023)

Access, correct or delete your data anytime by emailing our support address in the footer. We respond within 72 hours.

Data retention

Order records are kept for 3 years for tax compliance, then anonymised.
